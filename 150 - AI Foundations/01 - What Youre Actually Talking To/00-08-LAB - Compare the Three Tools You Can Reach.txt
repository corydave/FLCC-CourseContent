^^ LAB - Compare the Three Tools You Can Reach

## Description

Three numbers explain most of the frustration people have with these tools: the *context window*, the *knowledge cutoff*, and the *rate limit*. This lab is where you go and find two of them yourself, on tools you can actually get to, and then watch what happens at the edge of one.

## What to do

1. Pick three AI tools you have access to. Free accounts are fine and are the point. If you want to use more than three services, go for it!

2. For each, find and write down: the cost, the knowledge cutoff if it will tell you, and one thing it refuses to do.

3. Ask all three the same question about something recent in your field.

4. Note which hedged, which refused, and which answered confidently.

## What to submit

A one-page comparison, in whatever format suits you - a table, a list, three short paragraphs. Plus your note on step 4.

* Three tools you can actually reach, with the cost and the cutoff you found - or an honest *it would not tell me*, which is itself a finding.

* One mistake you found. It's okay if there wasn't any mistake.

* Your note on step 4: who hedged, who refused, who answered confidently.
